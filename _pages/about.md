**翁嘉思（Jiasi Weng）**，广州大学计算机科学与网络工程学院副教授。研究方向为可信机器学习、隐私保护、可验证计算与区块链。

**Jiasi Weng** is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Cyber Engineering at [Guangzhou University](https://www.gzhu.edu.cn/). She works on trustworthiness-enhancing techniques for collaborative machine learning, including privacy protection, verifiable computation, incentive mechanisms, and blockchain.

## 简介 / Bio

翁嘉思于 2026 年加入广州大学任副教授。2021 年于暨南大学获信息安全博士学位，导师为翁健教授，博士论文为《基于区块链的安全可信协作机器学习关键技术研究》。2016–2021 年在暨南大学硕博连读；2012–2016 年于华南农业大学获软件工程学士学位。2023 年 3 月至 2026 年 3 月任暨南大学网络空间安全学院副研究员。2021 年 12 月至 2022 年 7 月在香港城市大学计算机系从事博士后研究（合作导师：王聪教授），2019 年 10 月至 2020 年 10 月在该系担任研究助理。

Jiasi Weng joined Guangzhou University as an Associate Professor in 2026. She received her Ph.D. in Information Security from Jinan University in 2021, advised by Prof. Jian Weng. From March 2023 to March 2026, she was an Associate Researcher at the College of Cyber Security, Jinan University. From December 2021 to July 2022, she was a postdoctoral researcher supervised by Prof. Cong Wang at City University of Hong Kong, where she also worked as a research assistant from October 2019 to October 2020.

She has published in IEEE TIFS, IEEE TDSC, IEEE TMC, IEEE TNNLS, and IEEE INFOCOM. Her paper *DeepChain* (IEEE TDSC, 2021) was selected as an ESI Highly Cited Paper and Hot Paper.

## 研究方向 / Research Interests

- 可信协作机器学习 / Trustworthy collaborative machine learning
- 隐私保护联邦学习 / Privacy-preserving federated learning
- 可验证计算与模型测试 / Verifiable computation and model testing
- 机器遗忘与模型审计 / Machine unlearning and model auditing
- 区块链激励与去中心化机器学习 / Blockchain incentives and decentralized ML
